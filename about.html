WELCOME TO THE KINGDOM OF LESOTHO

Lesotho is famously known as the "Kingdom in the Sky" because of its high mountains and beautiful landscapes. The country offers amazing mountain views, rich cultural heritage and exciting outdoor activities for tourists.

Maletsunyane Falls

Maletsunyane Falls is one of the most beautiful waterfalls in Lesotho. It is about 192 metres high and attracts many visitors. Tourists can enjoy pony trekking and other outdoor activities.

Thaba-Bosiu

Thaba-Bosiu is an important historical place in Lesotho. It was used as a natural fortress by King Moshoeshoe I and played an important role in the history of the Basotho nation. Visitors can also learn about Basotho culture and history.

Sani Pass

Sani Pass is a beautiful mountain road connecting South Africa to the highlands of Lesotho. It is famous for its winding roads, mountain scenery and Sani Mountain Lodge, which is home to the highest pub in Africa.

There are many more beautiful places to visit in Lesotho. Come and experience the mountains, culture and natural beauty of the Mountain Kingdom.
